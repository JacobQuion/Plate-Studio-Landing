Frequently asked questions

Who is Plate Studio for?

Plate Studio is for busy restaurant owners who want professional video ads but don’t have the time, budget or skills to film and edit them. If you can run a kitchen, you can run a campaign.

What makes Plate Studio unique?

It’s built only for restaurants and turns your real reviews and photos into a finished video ad from a single link. No tech skills needed: edit with simple sliders or by telling the AI, then publish everywhere in one click.

Why can’t I just use ChatGPT or Claude to make a video?

General AI tools make you write the script, gather your reviews and photos, and format it yourself. Plate Studio does all of that for you automatically.

Where does the data come from?

Only from your Google reviews, Yelp page and the photos you upload, and you can edit everything before it goes live.

Investors: What would you spend venture funding on?

Better AI APIs for faster, higher-quality videos, and marketing so more restaurants know about Plate Studio.
