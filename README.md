## 
Most small restaurant owners have never made a video ad. [Building the app](https://github.com/JacobQuion/Plate-Studio-App) was one thing, but convincing busy owners that they could get a finished ad from just their phone photos and a Google Maps link was the real challenge. This landing page was designed to show the product working in seconds, so owners understand it before they ever sign up.

## **Highlights**
- Clean landing design following a hero -> how it works -> features -> FAQs format for high conversion rates.
- Animated "learns your restaurant" card with a live review counter and waveform, plus a mock editor that writes and renders an ad on screen.
- Scroll animations and a floating Create button that appears once the demo video reaches the middle of the screen.
- Separate pricing, contact, privacy and terms pages with full Open Graph previews for sharing.
